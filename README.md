# Slide Style Cloner v2.0 🎨

> **Autonomous Executive Presentation & Style Remake Engine: The Surgical In-Place Remake Protocol.**

`slide-style-cloner` is an enterprise-grade agent skill designed for AI pairs (Claude, Antigravity, Gemini CLI). It goes beyond simple text substitution or crude blank-canvas recreation. It preserves **100% of luxury 3D graphics, sophisticated color themes, and glassmorphism card layouts** from input PPTX/PDF templates while surgically removing external vendor logos, replacing mismatched stock photos with custom domain assets, and mapping full-length C-Suite executive narratives.

---

## ⚡ The 4 Master Protocols (Surgical In-Place Remake)

1. **Protocol 1: Vector Logo & Hyperlink Purge (벡터 로고 & 링크 완전 박멸)**:
   - Identifies and permanently deletes hidden vector logo shapes (e.g. `GROUP 300`) on Slide 1.
   - Cleans external commercial hyperlinks from `_rels` and sanitizes `docProps/core.xml` and `app.xml` metadata.
2. **Protocol 2: In-Place Media Replacement (인-플레이스 미디어 1:1 교체)**:
   - Directly overwrites files inside the PPTX ZIP package (`ppt/media/`) at the byte level.
   - Replaces mismatched stock photos with domain-specific C-level profile cards, dual synergy infographics, and patent tech diagrams without disturbing layout dimensions by even 1mm.
3. **Protocol 3: Full Narrative Mapping (30장 완결형 실전 내러티브 매핑)**:
   - Maps rigorous business logic (patents, unit economics, go-to-market roadmap) 1:1 into the template's visual hierarchy (Action Title, Kicker, Hero Metrics, Card Body).
4. **Protocol 4: Standard Packaging & Tri-Version Archive (`[001]` ~ `[999]`)**:
   - Organizes deliverables into 4 subfolders: `result/`, `images/`, `src/`, `jupyternotebook/`.
   - Simultaneously preserves 3 distinct PPTX versions: `v1.0 (Initial Clone)`, `v2.0 (12-Slide Executive Quick Pitch)`, and `v3.0 (Masterpiece Remake)`.
